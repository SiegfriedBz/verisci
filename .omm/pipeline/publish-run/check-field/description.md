Step check-field-<n> (workflows/src/publish/field-check.ts, core's fieldCheckPrompt and parseFieldAnswer): asks gemini-3.5-flash-lite, on the free tier, whether a new paper is in LabsTrail's field from its title, abstract and opening (ADR 0037). Outside ends the run refused: outside-field before anything is stored, logging Gemini's reason with the CID. A check that gets no answer (a rate limit, an outage, an answer that does not parse) waits 30 s (check-field-wait-<n>) and asks once more (check-field-<n>-again); a second miss publishes the paper, its rating checking the field again, and logs field-unchecked. Gemini refusing the request (a bad key, a retired model) fails the run as setup, with nothing stored. Asked once per run, on the first attempt whose parse-paper succeeds: a later attempt asks nothing, and a draft or a minted asset is never checked.
